About me

I am a master's student in Industrial Engineering at Koç University (Istanbul), advised by Prof. Sibel Salman and co-advised by Dr. Ali Hassanzadeh (Alliance Manchester Business School). My research brings together operations research and machine learning to make decisions that stay reliable under uncertainty.

My current work spans data-driven optimization, robust optimization, and decision-focused learning — from post-disaster network recovery under uncertain demand to predict-then-optimize pipelines. Earlier, as an undergraduate at Gebze Technical University, I developed GLORO, a bilevel, surrogate-based method for robust design optimization with expensive simulations.

I am especially drawn to data-driven and robust optimization, and I want to work on both their theoretical foundations and their real-world applications. I am currently applying to PhD programs (Fall 2027 entry) with the long-term goal of an academic career. If our research interests overlap, I would be glad to connect.

Optimization & resilience in post-disaster telecom infrastructure

Graduate Researcher · Koç University · 2025 – present · with S. Salman & A. Hassanzadeh

Deciding where to deploy mobile base stations (Cells on Wheels) to restore coverage after a disaster, when surviving infrastructure, road access, and demand are all uncertain. I compare scenario-based stochastic programming, robust worst-case optimization, and CVaR models, and extend them to a dynamic multi-period model in which units can be relocated over time. Tested on Istanbul's Avcılar district using Türk Telekom data.

Cells on Wheels (COWs) Robust & stochastic programming CVaR Manuscript in preparation

Distributionally robust optimization for the newsvendor problem

Independent Study (INDR 599) · Koç University · with Prof. Fikri Karaesmen · 2026

A theoretical study of how the classical newsvendor problem changes when the demand distribution itself is uncertain. I build ambiguity sets around the empirical distribution using Wasserstein and Kolmogorov–Smirnov distances, then use strong duality to reduce the distributionally robust problem to tractable forms — for instance, recovering the sample-average solution plus an interpretable regularization term. The work ties together DRO, optimal transport, and data-driven decision-making.

Distributionally robust optimization Wasserstein / KS ambiguity Newsvendor · SAA

GLORO — robust airfoil design via a bilevel, surrogate-based method

Undergraduate Researcher · Gebze Technical University · 2024 – 2025 · Advisor: F. Öztoprak · TÜBİTAK 2209-B

Developed GLORO, a bilevel methodology for robust optimization with expensive function evaluations, pairing Gaussian Process surrogates and Bayesian optimization for uncertainty quantification (lower level) with local model-based search in the design space (upper level). Applied to robust airfoil shape design (RAE 2822), optimizing the shape for a robust lift-to-drag ratio under uncertainty in Mach number and angle of attack.
